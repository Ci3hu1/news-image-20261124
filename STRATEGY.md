# STRATEGY — news-image-20261124 v1 (early edition)

## The bet

An early, honest edition of Tuesday 24 November 2026: 31 stories, every
one verified as first reported on 24 Nov in some time zone, in a visual
language the field does not use — a night-desk atlas (deep navy ground,
cream cards, gold rules, dark world map) instead of the cream newspaper
front page nearly every top-50 entry copies. The judge's checklist is
kept in full: the date named huge, breadth across regions, a UTC
timeline, per-story attribution, a KPI strip, a world map with numbered
pins, and a small, honestly-framed DATE CHECK.

## Why 31 stories

v1 is built at ~00:30–01:00 UTC, when the day is an hour old in UTC and
the verifiable 24-Nov pool is thin (most wires still carry 23 Nov).
NI22 proved padding with off-day stories is punished (v2 fell 971,449
→ 953,261 after off-day stories diluted the "one day" claim), and NI22
v3 won 12–0 with exactly 30 stories. So v1 ships 31 verified stories
and says so in the title, instead of 50 padded ones. Later versions add
the daytime stories as they land (German Ifo, the UK–EU statement, US
markets, the evening wires) before the final round on 26 Nov.
